Leagues

Keep the group
in the game.

A shared home for the golfers who keep coming back.

Organize your League, invite members, and play games in that League’s context. Completed League games contribute to its standings, so your group can follow how the season is taking shape.

Quick Games stay separate from League standings. Your personal round history keeps your golf activity together.

Birdie Board

Every hole.
A new reason
to come back.

Turn qualifying birdies at your chosen course into a season-long challenge. See the holes you’ve completed, the ones still waiting, and the group’s progress.

Boards have their own course, tee, membership, and qualifying-round rules. Only eligible rounds contribute.
