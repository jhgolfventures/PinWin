01

Start

Make it your game.

Choose a Quick Game or a League game, pick your format, and select the course and tees. Bring in your golfers and agree on the rules before play begins.

Explore the Formats →
02

Play & score

One hole at a time.

Enter scores as you play and follow the competition. Keep score entry focused, with the round’s standings available when you want the wider picture.

Solo keeps the focus on your own score. Singles, Four-Ball, and Skins each bring their own competition to the round.

03

Review

Finish with the full picture.

Review the scores and finalize the round. A completed Game Sheet brings the scorecard and relevant competition results together, so you can return to them later.

04

Settle

Make the amounts clear.

Competitive results can create amounts owed between golfers. PinWin keeps those records together and tracks reported outside payments and receiver confirmations.

You pay each other directly. PinWin does not collect entry fees, hold funds, or transfer payments.

Good to know

A few clubhouse questions.

Do I have to play for money?

No. Solo is score-only, and competitive game stakes are chosen during setup.

Can I play outside a League?

Yes. Quick Games give you a way to play with registered golfers without putting that game into a League’s standings.

Does PinWin handle payments?

No. Players pay directly outside PinWin. The app records obligations and payment confirmations.
