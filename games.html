Your game lineup

Same group.
Pick your game.

Nine ways to play. Choose an old favourite, mix up the regular round, or give your group something new to talk about.

01SOLO

Solo

A little time for your own game.

Keep your own score, find your rhythm, and leave with a record of the round. Sometimes the only competition you need is the course in front of you.

Your own ball · Your own scorecard

1v1SINGLES MATCH PLAY

Singles Match Play

One friend. One match.

Go head to head, hole by hole. There’s always another chance to turn the match—and another story waiting at the clubhouse.

Individual match play · Gross or net

2v2FOUR-BALL MATCH PLAY

Four-Ball Match Play

A good partner changes the game.

Two golfers on each side, each playing their own ball. The lower partner score counts on each hole. Share the pressure, enjoy the good shots, and keep your team in it.

Two against two · Lower partner score

01SKINS

Skins

One hole can change the afternoon.

Compete for individual holes, with a skin on the line each time. Agree on gross or net scoring and carryovers before you tee off, then let the round unfold.

Individual holes · Gross or net

18INDIVIDUAL STROKE PLAY

Individual Stroke Play

Every shot has its place.

Play your own ball and count the strokes across the round. A familiar test, a clear total, and plenty to talk about when the last putt drops.

Total strokes · Individual competition

PTSSTABLEFORD

Stableford

Give every good hole its due.

Play for points against a target score on each hole. Keep chasing the next good result, with a fresh opportunity waiting on the next tee.

Points scoring · Hole by hole

2/4SCRAMBLE

Scramble

Find the best shot. Go together.

Choose a two- or four-player team. Everyone hits, the team chooses its preferred ball, and you play on from there. A round where everybody can have their moment.

Two or four players · Shared team score

TEAMTEAM BEST BALL

Team Best Ball

Your own ball. A shared result.

Each golfer plays their own ball, with the team’s best score on each hole counting toward its round. Bring your good holes. Let your partners bring theirs.

Team stroke play · Best score counts

ROTLONE RANGER

Lone Ranger

A different golfer in the spotlight.

Take turns carrying the Lone Ranger role as your team works through the round. The responsibility moves around, and everyone gets a turn when it matters.

Team rotation · Shared competition

Set the terms.
Then enjoy the golf.

Scoring method, tees, stakes, and applicable side-game rules belong in setup. Make sure everyone understands the game before the first score.

From match play and team rounds to points and total strokes, agree on the format and rules before the first tee. PinWin brings the round into focus.
