Four ways to play

A game for
your kind of round.

Start with a familiar format. Set the rules together. Let the competition unfold.

01SOLO

Solo

Your round. Your record.

Keep your own score and revisit your completed rounds. A simple way to focus on the course and your game.

For your own scorecard and round history.

1v1SINGLES

Singles

One golfer against another.

Play a head-to-head match, follow the position as the round unfolds, and review the result together.

Individual match play · Gross or net scoring

2v2FOUR-BALL

Four-Ball

A partner makes it interesting.

Two golfers on each side. Each plays their own ball; the lower partner score counts for the team on each hole.

Team match play · Two golfers per side

1SKINS

Skins

Every hole has something to play for.

Compete for individual holes. Set the scoring and carryover rules before the first tee, then follow the skins as you play.

Individual holes · Gross or net competition

Set the terms.
Then enjoy the golf.

Scoring method, tees, stakes, and applicable side-game rules belong in setup. Make sure everyone understands the game before the first score.

Choose Solo, Singles, Four-Ball, or Skins. Each format has its own setup and results, with the same clear record of your round.
